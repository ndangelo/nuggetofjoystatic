How To Run a Great X/Bluesky Contest

A good X contest or giveaway should be simple, easy to understand, visually engaging, and designed as a campaign—not just one post.

For this assignment, you will create an 8-post X contest campaign. Each post should have a different purpose while maintaining a consistent visual style.

Before creating your campaign, review X’s current contest guidelines

https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-contest-rules

1. Start With a Goal

First, decide what your contest is supposed to accomplish.

Possible goals include:

  • Increase engagement
  • Promote a product
  • Build brand awareness
  • Generate user-created content
  • Increase followers
  • Drive traffic to a website

Example

Goal: Increase engagement with college-age sneaker fans while promoting a new sneaker.

The goal should influence everything else you create.

2. Know Your Audience

Be specific about who you are trying to reach.

Too broad:
College students

Better:
College students ages 18–24 who are interested in sneakers, fashion, and streetwear.

Your prize should also make sense for your audience.

Example

Contest: Design Your Dream Sneaker

Prize: The winning sneaker design will actually be produced.

The prize gives the audience a reason to participate because it is directly connected to their interests.

3. Keep the Rules Simple

People should be able to understand how to enter almost immediately.

Example

HOW TO ENTER

  1. Create your sneaker design.
  2. Post it on X.
  3. Mention @YourBrand.
  4. Use #DreamSneakerContest.

Avoid making the entry process unnecessarily complicated.

4. Create a Campaign — Not Just One Post

Your eight posts should work together as a campaign.

Don’t simply make eight versions of the same advertisement.

Instead, give each post a different job:

Launch → Prize → Instructions → Engagement → User Content → Behind the Scenes → Reminder → Final Call

Post 1: Launch

Purpose

Introduce the contest and immediately communicate:

  • What the contest is
  • What the prize is
  • How long people have to enter

Example

CONTEST TIME!

Design your dream sneaker for a chance to have your design produced.

Enter by October 15.

#DreamSneakerContest

Real X Example

This Throne giveaway is a good example of a launch post because the giveaway, prize, and limited time are immediately communicated.

What to notice

  • The giveaway is immediately obvious.
  • The prize is identified quickly.
  • The time limit creates urgency.
  • The visual supports the announcement.

Your post

Create the launch post for your own contest.

Post 2: Prize Spotlight

Purpose

Focus entirely on the prize.

The audience should understand why the prize is worth entering for.

Example

YOUR DESIGN. YOUR COLORS. YOUR SNEAKER.

One winner gets their sneaker design produced.

Real X Example

This giveaway makes the product itself the focus of the post. The prize is visually prominent and easy to identify.

What to notice

  • Is the prize obvious within a few seconds?
  • Does the image make the prize desirable?
  • Is the text supporting the image rather than competing with it?

Your post

Create a strong visual presentation of your contest prize.

Post 3: How to Enter

Purpose

Clearly explain exactly what participants need to do.

Example

CREATE → POST → TAG → ENTER

  1. Create your sneaker design.
  2. Post it on X.
  3. Mention @YourBrand.
  4. Use #DreamSneakerContest.

Real X Example

This LETSHUOER post provides a clear example of communicating participation requirements. It tells people what to do and uses the graphic to provide additional information.

What to notice

  • The instructions are easy to scan.
  • The required actions are clearly separated.
  • The audience knows what to do without reading a long paragraph.

Your post

Make your entry requirements easy to understand at a glance.

Post 4: Engagement

Purpose

Get people talking about the contest or the product.

The audience doesn’t always need to enter the contest in every post. Sometimes you simply want them to interact with the campaign.

Example

Which color combination would you choose?

Green + White
Blue + Orange
Purple + Yellow
Black + Red

Tell us below.

Real X Example

This Clawi.ai giveaway uses a question to encourage people to respond in the comments. The interaction itself becomes part of the entry process.

What to notice

  • The question is easy to answer.
  • The audience is given a specific action.
  • The question relates directly to the campaign.
  • The post encourages conversation rather than simply repeating the contest announcement.

Your post

Create a question or interaction that relates directly to your contest.

Post 5: User-Generated Content

Purpose

Ask your audience to create and share something.

This makes the audience part of the campaign instead of simply asking them to consume advertising.

Example

SHOW US YOUR DESIGN!

Post your dream sneaker and mention @YourBrand.

Your design could become our next custom sneaker.

Real X Example

This Manic Fanart Contest is a useful example because participants are asked to create their own video, photo, or thread as their contest entry.

What to notice

  • The audience is asked to create something.
  • Participants have multiple ways to express their idea.
  • The user-created content becomes part of the campaign.
  • The post clearly explains what participants are expected to make.

Your post

Ask your audience to create, photograph, design, or share something related to your contest.

Post 6: Behind the Scenes

Purpose

Give people something different from another promotional graphic.

Show how the product, prize, or campaign is being created.

Example

FROM SKETCH TO SNEAKER

Show a short video of a designer sketching several sneaker concepts and developing the final design.

Caption:

Here’s how a sneaker goes from an idea to a finished product.

Real X Example

This GMMTV post is explicitly presented as Behind The Scenes and uses video to show material that audiences would not normally see.

What to notice

  • The content gives the audience access to something happening behind the scenes.
  • Video makes the post feel different from a traditional advertisement.
  • The content adds personality to the campaign.
  • The post shows that behind-the-scenes content doesn’t have to directly sell the product.

Your post

Create a short video that shows something happening behind the scenes of your contest, product, or campaign.

Post 7: Reminder

Purpose

Remind people that the contest is still happening.

A reminder should feel like a new communication—not simply a copy of the launch post.

Example

3 DAYS LEFT!

Have you entered yet?

#DreamSneakerContest

Real X Example

This Throne post uses a limited-time window to create urgency and encourage people to act before the giveaway ends.

What to notice

  • The remaining time is immediately visible.
  • The post creates urgency.
  • The message is different from the original launch post.

Your post

Create a reminder post using a countdown or another visual indication of time.

Post 8: Final Call

Purpose

Create a final sense of urgency immediately before the contest closes.

Example

LAST CHANCE!

The #DreamSneakerContest closes tonight at 11:59 p.m.

Enter before it’s too late.

Real X Example

This Beyond the Verse post makes the final day of the contest the central message and clearly communicates what happens next.

What to notice

  • The deadline is prominent.
  • The language creates urgency.
  • The audience knows exactly what they need to do next.

Your post

Make the deadline impossible to miss.

Keep the Campaign Consistent

Although each post should have a different purpose, the campaign should look like it belongs together.

Define these before designing your posts:

  • Topic
  • Goal
  • Target audience
  • Theme
  • Voice/tone
  • Color palette
  • Typography
  • Graphic style
  • Hashtag
  • Prize
  • Contest dates
  • Entry requirements

Example Campaign Style

Campaign: Dream Sneaker Contest

Colors: Black, white, neon green

Tone: Energetic and youthful

Typography: Bold sans-serif

Graphic style: High-contrast photography with large typography

Hashtag: #DreamSneakerContest

The eight posts should look like parts of the same campaign, not eight unrelated designs.

Assignment

Create an 8-post X contest campaign.

Campaign Plan

Before designing your posts, define:

  1. Topic
  2. Goal
  3. Target audience
  4. Theme
  5. Voice/tone
  6. Color palette
  7. Typography
  8. Graphic style
  9. Hashtag
  10. Prize
  11. Contest dates
  12. Entry requirements

Create 8 Posts

Your campaign must include:

  1. Launch
  2. Prize Spotlight
  3. How to Enter
  4. Engagement
  5. User-Generated Content
  6. Behind the Scenes
  7. Reminder
  8. Final Call

Format Requirements

  • 6 static/image-based posts
  • 2 video-based posts
  • Each post should have a different purpose.
  • The campaign should have a consistent visual style.
  • Entry requirements should be clear.
  • The campaign should have a clear beginning, middle, and end.

Final Submission

Before submitting, check that:

  • All 8 posts are included.
  • 6 posts are static/image-based.
  • 2 posts are video-based.
  • The visual style is consistent.
  • Each post has a different purpose.
  • The contest rules are easy to understand.
  • The prize is clearly communicated.
  • The beginning, middle, and end of the campaign are clear.
